Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about bulk downloading image assets.

Is this online image downloader free?

Yes. The web tool at imagedownloader.org is completely free, with no signup required and no daily constraints on individual files download.

Does it work on all websites?

It works on almost all public-facing webpages. However, some protected, paywalled, or login-required pages (like private social feeds or admin dashboards) cannot be scraped via the online tool. For those, we recommend installing our browser extension, which runs directly inside your browser window.

What image formats does it support?

It extracts and supports popular web image formats including JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, SVG, BMP, and ICO. You can choose to download all files or filter by type.

How many images can I download at once?

Our online bulk tool does not have any strict limits. However, download zip requests larger than 100 images can take a few seconds to compile depending on your connection speed. We throttle fetches internally to prevent rate-limiting by hosts.

Do I need to create an account?

No, there is no email registration or account required. Paste the URL, filter the files, and download what you need instantly.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. We do not store or track any URLs you paste, nor do we host or look at your images. The entire extraction and downloading process happens locally on your computer via client-side JavaScript execution.
