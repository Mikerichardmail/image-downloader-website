Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 22, 2026

At imagedownloader.org, we take the privacy of our visitors and users seriously. This Privacy Policy details how information is handled across our website and browser extensions (ImageMaster).

1. No Personal Data Collection

Our online bulk image downloader tool and browser extensions do not collect, monitor, store, or transmit any personal data. All image scanning, scraping, size detection, and packaging (ZIP compiling) functions are executed locally inside your web browser on your machine. The URLs you submit to our online downloader are processed serverless-side in real-time solely to bypass origin CORS blocks and are never logged or saved to any database.

2. Browser Extensions Data Handling

The ImageMaster browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox run entirely in your local browser tab sandbox. Our extensions do not track your browsing history, monitor keystrokes, log active cookies, or send information about the pages you visit to any external servers. Your settings preferences are saved locally on your device via standard browser storage settings.

3. Third-Party Payments (Gumroad)

Transactions for purchasing an ImageMaster Pro license key are processed securely by Gumroad. When buying Pro, Gumroad handles your name, email address, and billing details. We do not have access to, nor do we store, your credit card details or bank account information. You can review Gumroad's Privacy Policy for more details on their encryption practices.

4. Cookies and Web Analytics

Our website utilizes minor server analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to monitor aggregate traffic statistics (e.g. daily visitors counts, general country regions, device browser types). These systems do not track individual users across different domains or compile personal profiles. We do not use third-party advertising tracking pixels (like Google Ads or Meta Pixel).

5. Children's Privacy

Our services do not collect personal data from anyone under the age of 13. Since our website and extensions do not collect personal data at all, we are fully compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) guidelines.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to align with browser store updates or server configurations. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with the modified revision date.

7. Contact Support

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy policies, please contact us via email at admin@imagedownloader.org.
