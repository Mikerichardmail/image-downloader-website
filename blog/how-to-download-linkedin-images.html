How to Download Images from LinkedIn Posts and Profiles

Published: May 2026 | Written by ConverterWordToPDF Labs Editorial Team

LinkedIn is the leading professional networking platform, housing millions of high-quality business infographics, graphic layouts, professional headshots, and corporate articles. However, LinkedIn takes extensive measures to protect user-uploaded media from being copied. Right-click saving is often disabled or intercepted on articles and member profile pictures, making manual downloads difficult.

Fortunately, because a browser extension operates locally inside your browser window, it inherits your login credentials and active session, allowing you to extract these visual assets safely and legally without sharing sensitive credentials. In this guide, we will show you how to download high-resolution graphics from LinkedIn articles, posts, and profiles.

Why Remote Cloud Scrapers Fail on LinkedIn

If you try to use an online web downloader to scrape a LinkedIn page, it will fail because LinkedIn blocks remote bots and requires authentication gates. Since cloud scrapers run on remote servers, they lack the active session keys needed to load member feeds.

A local browser extension resolves this constraint because it acts inside your browser, running with the permissions of your active page tab. It parses the page DOM, locates the image source paths, and downloads the original files locally, preserving full account security.

Step-by-Step LinkedIn Image Extraction Guide

Follow these steps to extract visual assets from any LinkedIn feed post or article:

  1. Navigate to the LinkedIn feed, article, or profile containing the media you wish to save.
  2. Click the **ImageMaster** icon on your browser toolbar to open the scanning grid.
  3. The extension will scan the page elements, bypass the mouse click blockers, and display the professional headshots or infographics in the preview dashboard.
  4. Select the desired graphics and click **Download Selected** to save them locally.
