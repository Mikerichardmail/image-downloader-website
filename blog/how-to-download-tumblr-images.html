How to Download Tumblr Images and Photo Sets

Published: May 2026 | Written by ConverterWordToPDF Labs Editorial Team

Tumblr remains a popular microblogging platform for artists, photographers, and visual creators who share mood boards, illustrations, and animated GIFs. However, Tumblr posts often group multiple images together inside dynamic "photosets". Saving a complete photoset manually is extremely tedious, as you have to expand the post, open each image in a new tab, and save it individually.

Furthermore, Tumblr heavily compresses the images displayed on the main dashboard to save bandwidth. In this guide, we will explain how to extract high-resolution photosets and animated GIFs from any Tumblr blog or archive page instantly.

The Architecture of Tumblr Media

Tumblr hosts its visual assets on specialized media subdomains (like `media.tumblr.com`). When a user uploads a high-resolution graphic, Tumblr saves multiple resolutions, including `500`, `1280`, and the original master quality. On the dashboard, it displays the smaller `500` or `250` thumbnail resolutions.

To save the highest quality, you must access the `1280` or raw original file paths. ImageMaster's built-in Tumblr resolver automatically intercepts these media elements, queries the Tumblr post DOM, and rewrites the source paths to pull the uncompressed original assets.

Step-by-Step Tumblr Media Download Guide

Follow these steps to scrape and download photosets and GIFs in bulk:

  1. Navigate to the Tumblr blog, dashboard feed, or the specific archive page (`/archive`) you want to scrape.
  2. Launch the **ImageMaster** extension from your browser toolbar.
  3. Adjust the dimension sliders (e.g., setting the minimum height/width to 300px) to filter out small interface icons, blog logos, and avatars.
  4. Select all target photosets or animated GIFs, and click Download Selected to save them inside a ZIP folder.
