How to Download All Pinterest Board Images at Once

Published: May 2026 | Written by ConverterWordToPDF Labs Editorial Team

Pinterest is the ultimate search directory for design inspiration, color palettes, and visual references. Many designers and creators organize their projects by saving pins to dedicated Pinterest boards. But when you need to move those mockups into a design program like Figma, Photoshop, or Canva, downloading dozens of pins one by one is incredibly time-consuming.

Pinterest's dynamic interface makes traditional bulk saving difficult because the page utilizes lazy-loading grids. In this guide, we will show you how to use a bulk downloader extension to extract every single image from an entire Pinterest board in a single click, saving hours of manual labor.

Understanding Pinterest's Dynamic Feed Grid

Pinterest uses an infinite-scroll, masonry-style grid layout. To maintain high performance, the webpage only renders the HTML elements that are currently visible inside your screen's viewport. As you scroll down, new pins are loaded, and the pins that have scrolled far off-screen are temporarily removed from the Document Object Model (DOM).

A standard web crawler will fail to capture the entire board because it only sees the few pins loaded at the top. To scrape a large board successfully, your downloader needs an engine that can read the viewport cache or scroll the page automatically to capture loaded assets.

Step-by-Step Board Scraping Workflow

Follow these steps to compile and download all images from a public or private Pinterest board:

  1. Navigate to your target Pinterest board in your browser.
  2. Scroll down through the board at a steady pace to let the browser's dynamic cache register all the pins you want to download. For very large boards, enable the **Auto-Scroll** feature inside ImageMaster Pro.
  3. Once the page cache is populated, click the **ImageMaster** extension icon in your toolbar.
  4. The extension's built-in Pinterest resolver will automatically rewrite the low-quality feed links into their high-resolution master counterparts (`/originals/`).
  5. Review the visual grid, verify the resolution, click **Select All**, and choose **Download as ZIP** to export the board inside a single organized archive.

Leveraging Smart Filters for Organized Assets

To ensure your downloaded folder is clean, adjust the extension's filter sliders. Setting the minimum dimensions to 400px width will instantly exclude user profile avatars, site icons, and decorative layout buttons, leaving you with only clean pin designs.
