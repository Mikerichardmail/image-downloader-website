About Us

Published: May 2026

Our Mission

At imagedownloader.org, our mission is to build the fastest, most reliable, and privacy-focused asset collection tools for digital creators. Whether you are a web developer looking for layout references, a designer building mood boards, or a researcher compiling dataset visual matches, our suite of tools is designed to simplify your workflow.

Web browser standards have evolved, making it increasingly tedious to inspect elements, copy background images, or download multiple items from galleries. We want to remove this friction by building tools that inspect page source contexts automatically and package them for download in one click.

Who Built This?

imagedownloader.org and the ImageMaster browser extensions are developed and maintained by ConverterWordToPDF Labs. We are a small team of independent developers dedicated to building lightweight, functional utilities that make daily browsing tasks simple and safe.

Our Core Values

  • Privacy First: We do not track what websites you scrape or what images you download. The tool runs locally in your web browser. Any serverless backend APIs we deploy only act as direct pass-through proxies and do not store logs of requests.
  • Simplicity: We believe utility interfaces should be clean, functional, and devoid of bloated ad banners or forced signup screens.
  • Support for Open Web Standards: We build tools that run natively across all modern web browsers including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Brave.

Contact Us

Have questions, feature requests, or business inquiries? You can reach us directly via email at admin@imagedownloader.org or visit our dedicated Contact Page. We read every email and aim to respond to support inquiries within 24-48 hours.
